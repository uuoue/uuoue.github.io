## 模板引用

php里输入调用代码，改一下路径

![image-20261025104103179](../images/image-20261025104103179.png)

在smarty/templates/路径下创造index.tpl文件

![image-20261025104000829](../images/image-20261025104000829.png)

访问成功![image-20261025104117853](../images/image-20261025104117853.png)
